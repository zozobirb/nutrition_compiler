# nutrition_compiler

I The Purpose:
The purpose of this compiler is to generate Nutrition facts based on the user’s input of, a recipe, signifying multiple food items, or a single food item's nutrition facts. Script/Language of the input, requires:
-A measurement of a particular food item
-Specification of raw or wet, or rather the state of measurement
-Servings if any (none given default to one)
Then users can filter nutrition facts, by identifying what facts they want display, using the keyword: Macros, and specify what they want to see, if none specified, all are shown

Nutrition_compuler however, is only phase one of a bigger project, that I plan on using to generate pleasing UI results, rather than just textual output!!

This is useful, for people who don’t want to manually look up each calorie, or nutritional info of a particular recipe or item, only if they already know it, can they input it, and the compiler will output the resulting information. As we know many recipes exclude such important information, especially people with weight goals, or to an adherence to a diet. 

II Prototyping Input/Output:
Compilation Input:
1)
Recipe:
-80g of ground chicken
-56g of dry pasta
-¾ cup of tomato sauce
-120g of frozen veggies
Macros =>Protein,Carbs,Calories
Nutritious(?)

Output:
Recipes Nutrition Break Down:
Protein: 35g
Carbs: 25g
Calories: 365 calories

Overall:
Nutritious - Yes, overall, good source of protein, and low-calorie

2)
Food item:
80g of cooked Ground Turkey
Macros =>Protein
Output:
